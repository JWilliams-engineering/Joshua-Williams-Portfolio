Engineering Internship · Insteel Wire Products

Production Component Redesign & Failure Analysis

Project Overview

As an Engineering Intern at Insteel Wire Products (May 2025 - Present), I ran full engineering cycles on production components: failure analysis, CAD redesign in Fusion 360, and physical testing. This work reduced costs on select components by up to 97%, generating over $10,000 in annual savings, and cut part defects and failures by more than 50% through structured FMEA.

My Role & Approach

Produced 100+ 2D technical drawings in AutoCAD and developed 75+ 3D CAD components. Conducted FMEA on manufacturing processes to identify failure modes and implement corrective actions. Authored a Quality Control Training Guide and revised SOPs and LOTO procedures (OSHA 1910.147) to standardize testing protocols, reduce operator error, and maintain safety compliance.

Tools & Methods

Fusion 360 (CAD) AutoCAD FMEA / RCA SOP Writing LOTO Compliance (OSHA 1910.147) Quality Control
